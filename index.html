WELCOME TO MARBL HEARTH

Food made with
care & character.

Marbl Hearth is a Harare bakery and catering business serving fresh lunches, takeaway meals, pies, snacks, celebration cakes and catering for special occasions.

From beautifully finished birthday cakes to savoury platters and everyday meals, every order is prepared with attention to presentation, flavour and the occasion it is made for.

Whether you're stopping in for something quick or planning a celebration, Marbl Hearth brings food and presentation together in one warm, personal experience.
